Small language models, trained from scratch on a tight budget.

Own weights, own tokenizer, own data pipeline, no fine-tuned base. It started on an 8 GB laptop GPU; the current model is a 1B mixture of experts that cost under $20 to train on one rented GPU.

498,813 of 638,342 teacher answers passed in a sandbox. MBPP went from 1.9% to 7.4%.
