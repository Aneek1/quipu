## Outcome

Two sets have terms that allow training a model with public weights: GNHK (CC BY 4.0) and Bentham ICFHR 2014 (CC BY 4.0), both with attribution in the model card. IAM, CVL and Imgur5K are non-commercial and give no clear permission for public derived weights, so they are evaluation only; of these only IAM is line-level, so IAM lines are the v1 handwriting evaluation set (subject to registration and a re-check of its page, which could not be loaded live today). RIMES is French and Washington's terms could not be read, so neither is used. v1 therefore trains on synthetic lines plus GNHK and Bentham line crops, and reports handwriting accuracy on the IAM line test split, kept out of training.
